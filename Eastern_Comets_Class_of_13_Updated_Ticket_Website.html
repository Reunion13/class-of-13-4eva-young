A message to our classmates

Welcome Back, Class of 2013

Dear Eastern Comets, Class of 2013,

Can you believe how far we've come? It feels like just yesterday we were walking the halls, heading to class, laughing with our friends, making memories, and counting down the days until graduation. Back then, we were busy living each day without knowing just how much we would one day look back and treasure those moments.

We came together as classmates, but along the way we became part of something bigger. We shared school days, inside jokes, celebrations, challenges, friendships, and experiences that helped shape the people we are today. Some memories still make us laugh out loud. Some remind us of people and moments we wish we could revisit for just a little while. And some are the simple everyday things we never realized would mean so much.

Since graduation, life has taken each of us on a different journey. We've grown, changed, chased dreams, faced unexpected turns, celebrated milestones, and learned lessons we couldn't have imagined back then. Some of us have stayed close, some have lost touch, and some may not have seen one another in years. But no matter how much time has passed or how far away life has carried us, we will always share one thing: we are part of the Eastern Comets Class of 2013.

This reunion is about more than getting together for a day. It's about reconnecting with familiar faces, putting names to memories, catching up on the chapters we've missed, and remembering the people we were when our paths first crossed. It's a chance to laugh about the old days, celebrate the lives we've built, honor the memories we carry, and make new ones together.

So come as you are. Bring your stories, your pictures, your laughter, and your Comet pride. Whether you were the life of every gathering or someone who quietly made their mark, you belong here. There is room for every classmate, every story, and every memory.

Let's take a little time to slow down, look around, and appreciate how special it is to come from the same place and share a chapter of life that will always connect us. We cannot turn back the clock, but we can come together and celebrate the moments that helped make us who we are.

Once a Comet, always a Comet. Class of '13 — 4eva Young! ☄
